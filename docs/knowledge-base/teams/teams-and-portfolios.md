# Teams & Portfolios

As your collection workload grows, teams and portfolios help you organise who handles what.

## Teams

Teams let you group your staff into working units.

### Creating a team
1. Give the team a name
2. Add members — individually or in bulk
3. Set the team status (active or inactive)

### Managing teams

| Action | What you can do |
|--------|----------------|
| **Add members** | Add individual users or bulk-add multiple members at once |
| **Remove members** | Remove users who are no longer on the team |
| **Assign portfolios** | Give the team responsibility for specific case portfolios |
| **Update status** | Set the team as active or inactive |
| **Delete team** | Remove the team entirely (cases are not affected) |

### How teams are used
- Assign portfolios to teams so each team knows which cases they're responsible for
- Track team performance — cases resolved, collection metrics
- Distribute workload across teams by portfolio

## Portfolios

Portfolios are groups of cases. You can organise them however makes sense for your business.

### Common ways to organise portfolios
- **By client** — all cases for a specific customer in one portfolio
- **By region** — cases grouped by geographic area
- **By risk level** — high-risk cases separated from routine ones
- **By value** — large debts in one portfolio, small debts in another
- **By stage** — new cases vs. cases in legal proceedings

### Creating a portfolio
1. Give the portfolio a name
2. Optionally assign it to a team
3. Associate cases with the portfolio

### Role-based access

| Role | What they see |
|------|-------------|
| **Admin** | All portfolios across the company |
| **Manager** | Portfolios assigned to their team |
| **User** | Portfolios assigned to them personally |

This ensures team members only see the cases they're responsible for, while managers and admins have broader oversight.

### Managing portfolios

| Action | What you can do |
|--------|----------------|
| **Associate cases** | Add cases to the portfolio |
| **Assign to team** | Give a team responsibility for this portfolio |
| **Update details** | Edit the portfolio name or description |
| **Delete portfolio** | Remove the portfolio (cases remain, they're just unassigned) |

## Performance reporting

Track how your teams and portfolios are performing:

### Team metrics
- **Cases resolved** — how many cases has the team closed
- **Collection rate** — what percentage of assigned invoices are being paid
- **Revenue generated** — total payments collected by the team

### Portfolio metrics
- **Outstanding balance** — total still owed across all cases in the portfolio
- **Case volume** — how many cases are in the portfolio
- **Workflow progress** — how many cases are actively progressing through workflows
- **Collection effectiveness** — payment rates for the portfolio

These metrics help you identify:
- Which teams are performing well and which may need support
- Which portfolios have the most outstanding debt
- Where to allocate resources for maximum collection effectiveness

## Good to know

- Teams and portfolios are **organisational tools** — they don't change how cases, invoices, or workflows behave
- A case can belong to one portfolio at a time
- A portfolio can be assigned to one team
- Deleting a team or portfolio doesn't affect the underlying cases — they remain in the system
- Only Admins can create and manage teams and portfolios
