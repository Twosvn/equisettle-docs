# Invoice Analytics

The invoice analytics page gives you a detailed view of your invoices — total amounts, payment trends, category breakdowns, and risk analysis.

## Total metrics

| Metric | What it shows |
|--------|-------------|
| **Total invoiced amount** | The sum of all invoice amounts |
| **Total paid amount** | How much has been collected |
| **Total unpaid amount** | How much is still owed on unpaid invoices |
| **Total partially paid amount** | Total on invoices with partial payments |
| **Total overdue amount** | The value of invoices that are past their due date |
| **Total outstanding balance** | The sum of all outstanding balances across all open invoices |

## Count metrics

| Metric | What it shows |
|--------|-------------|
| **Total invoices** | Number of invoices in the filtered view |
| **Paid invoices** | Number fully paid |
| **Unpaid invoices** | Number with no payment received |
| **Partially paid invoices** | Number with some but not full payment |
| **Overdue invoices** | Number past their due date |

## Performance metrics

| Metric | What it measures |
|--------|-----------------|
| **Collection rate** | % of invoices that are paid (by count) |
| **Payment efficiency** | % of total invoiced amount that's been collected |
| **Overdue rate** | % of unpaid invoices that are past due |
| **Average invoice amount** | Total invoiced / Number of invoices |

These help you understand not just how much you've collected, but how efficiently your collection process is working.

## Category breakdown

See your invoice data broken down by category:

- **Accounts receivable** — money owed for delivered goods or services
- **Expense** — expense-related invoices
- **Payment** — payment records
- **Other** — anything else

For each category, you'll see:
- Total amount
- Paid amount
- Unpaid amount
- Number of invoices
- Collection rate (% of amount collected)

This helps identify which types of invoices are easiest or hardest to collect.

## Monthly breakdown

A month-by-month view showing:

- Total invoiced per month
- Paid amount per month
- Unpaid amount per month
- Overdue amount per month
- Collection rate per month
- Number of invoices per month

Use this to spot trends — for example, if collection rates are improving over time, or if certain months consistently have more overdue invoices.

## Top invoices

The 10 largest invoices by amount, showing:

- Invoice number
- Amount
- Status (paid, unpaid, partially paid)
- Category
- Due date

This helps you focus on the high-value invoices that have the biggest impact on your outstanding balance.

## Risk analysis

Invoices are scored by risk level based on predictive analytics:

| Risk level | What it means |
|------------|--------------|
| **Low risk** | High likelihood of being paid on time |
| **Medium risk** | Some indicators suggest potential delays |
| **High risk** | Significant risk of non-payment |
| **Very high risk** | Strong indicators of default |

For each risk level, you'll see:
- Number of invoices
- Total amount at that risk level

This helps you prioritise collection efforts — focus on high-risk invoices before they become long-term problems.

## Filtering

All invoice analytics can be filtered by:

- **Period**: All time, this year, last 6 months, last 30 days, last year
- **Category**: Accounts receivable, expense, payment, other, or all

Filters apply to every section on the page — totals, breakdowns, and risk analysis all update together.

## What's excluded

To keep your analytics accurate, these invoices are **not included**:

- **Void** invoices — cancelled, no longer valid
- **Superseded** invoices — replaced by payment plans or updated invoices
- **Cancelled** invoices — no longer active
- **Closed** invoices — already finalised

Only active, open invoices contribute to your analytics totals. This prevents inflated numbers from dead-end invoices that will never be collected.
