# Monthly Statements

Monthly statements give your customers a clear summary of everything they owe — all outstanding invoices, amounts, and due dates in one document.

## What's in a statement

A monthly statement includes:

- **List of all outstanding invoices** — invoice number, amount, due date, and status for each
- **Total outstanding balance** — the sum of all unpaid amounts
- **Payment history** — a summary of recent payments received

This gives your customer a single, clear view of their account without needing to check each invoice individually.

## Setting up automatic statements

You can configure statements to be sent automatically for each customer:

1. Open the **customer agreement**
2. Navigate to **monthly statement settings**
3. Set the **schedule** — for example, the 1st of every month
4. Save — statements will be generated and sent automatically on that schedule

## Sending statements manually

You can also send a statement at any time:

1. Open the customer agreement
2. Choose **"Send statement"**
3. The statement is generated with current data and sent immediately

This is useful for:
- Mid-month check-ins
- Following up after a conversation with the customer
- Responding to a customer who asks "what do I owe?"

## Previewing statements

Before sending, you can preview the statement to check:

- Are all the right invoices included?
- Are the amounts correct?
- Is the total accurate?

This prevents sending a statement with errors or missing information.

## Statement history

View all previously sent statements for a customer:

- **Date sent** — when each statement was delivered
- **Content** — what was included in each statement

This provides a record of all communications sent and helps you track the frequency of statements.

## Good to know

- Statements are per customer agreement — they cover all invoices linked to that customer
- Only outstanding invoices are included (paid, void, and closed invoices are excluded)
- Statement settings are saved per customer — different customers can have different schedules
- Statements use your company branding
