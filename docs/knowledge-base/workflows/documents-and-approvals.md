# Documents & Approvals

Workflows often involve documents — requesting them from customers, generating them from templates, serving legal notices, and getting approvals before progressing.

## Document request stages

When a workflow stage requires documents from the customer:

### Setting up the request
1. Define which documents you need — each document has:
   - **Type** (e.g., Proof of Income, Identification, Financial Statement)
   - **Description** — what exactly you need
   - **Required** — whether the document is mandatory or optional

2. The customer receives a **secure link** to upload their documents
3. You can set a **reminder schedule** for non-responsive customers

### Tracking document status

| Status | What it means |
|--------|--------------|
| **Requested** | Document has been asked for but not yet received |
| **Uploaded** | Customer has uploaded the document |
| **Generated** | Document was auto-generated by the system |
| **Served** | Document has been formally served to the customer |
| **Acknowledged** | Customer has confirmed receipt |

### Verification
Once a document is uploaded:
1. An admin or manager **reviews** the document
2. They can **approve** (document is verified) or **reject** (customer needs to re-upload)
3. Rejected documents include a reason so the customer knows what to fix

## Document generation stages

When a workflow stage generates documents automatically:

1. Select a **document template** (pre-configured by your admin)
2. The system **fills in case data** — customer name, amounts, dates, company details
3. The generated document is:
   - Stored and available for download
   - Can be immediately used for **serving in the next stage**
   - Tracked with creation date and template used

### Common generated documents
- Payment agreements
- Debt acknowledgment letters
- Legal notices
- Settlement offers
- Statement of account

## Legal document serving

When a workflow stage involves formally serving legal documents:

### Serving methods

| Method | How it works |
|--------|-------------|
| **Email** | Document sent as an email attachment or link |
| **Post** | Document mailed physically — you record the tracking number and mail date |
| **Personal** | Document delivered in person — you record proof of service |

### Proof of service
For post and personal serving:
- Upload a **proof of service document** (PDF)
- Record the **date served** and **method used**
- For post: enter the **tracking number**

### Customer acknowledgment
- After serving, you can require the customer to **acknowledge receipt**
- The customer uses their secure portal link to confirm
- Acknowledgment date is recorded
- Status updates to "acknowledged"

## Approval workflows

Various actions in workflows require approval from managers or admins.

### Stage verification
Certain stages can be configured to require approval before the case can progress:

- Only specified roles (**Admin**, **Manager**) can approve
- Can require **documents to be present** before approval is granted
- Can require a **written reason** for the decision
- Approved stages proceed to the next step; rejected stages stay put

### Skip approvals
When someone wants to skip a stage:

1. The skip request is sent to authorised approvers
2. The approver reviews: why is the skip being requested?
3. They approve or reject with a reason
4. Approved: case advances past the stage
5. Rejected: case stays on the current stage

### Workflow transition approvals
When transitioning a case from one workflow to another:

1. The transition request goes to authorised approvers
2. The approver reviews the reason for the transition
3. Approved: new workflow starts
4. Rejected: case stays on the current workflow

## Audit trail

**Every action on every stage is recorded.** The audit trail includes:

| What's tracked | Details |
|---------------|---------|
| **Who** | User name, email, and role |
| **What** | Action taken (progressed, skipped, approved, rejected, document uploaded, etc.) |
| **When** | Timestamp |
| **Why** | Notes or reason provided |
| **Stage details** | Which stage, what type, what status |

### Stage history
Each case maintains a complete stage history showing:
- When each stage was entered
- What actions were taken during the stage
- When and how the stage was completed (auto-progress, manual, skip)
- Any notes or documents associated with the stage

This provides a complete, auditable record of how every case was handled — essential for compliance and internal review.

## Good to know

- Document templates are created and managed by Admins in the workflow settings
- Customer-facing document upload links are secure and time-limited
- Approval notifications are sent via email to the authorised approvers
- Rejected documents include the rejection reason, so customers know what to fix
- All approvals, rejections, and documents are part of the permanent audit trail
